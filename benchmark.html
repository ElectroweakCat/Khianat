Test it yourself

Watch Khianat solve mates and finish endgames, live on your device. Timings depend on your hardware, so this is for curiosity rather than for the record.

How it is measured

All tests run offline at a fixed search depth. That matters: the difficulty levels are defined by thinking time, so a fast computer would look stronger than a phone. Depth is the same everywhere and the opening randomisation is off, so a run can be repeated with the same result.

Tactics. Puzzles from the Lichess database, public domain and rated. The solve rates across the rating bands are fitted with the Elo formula, which gives a puzzle rating with a confidence range — the closest thing to a measured number this project has.
Forced mates. Mate in two and three. Pass or fail.
Endgame technique. Queen, rook, two rooks, pawn escort — against a random defence, with a move limit, once as White and once as Black.
Move quality. Khianat plays itself, Stockfish judges every move: how many centipawns did it give away?
Match play. Games against a rating limited Stockfish, colours alternating.

Stockfish runs offline only. It is GPL licensed and no part of this website, so nothing here makes the site bigger or slower.

What the numbers do not say

Solving puzzles is not the same as playing well. A puzzle announces that something is there to be found; a real game does not. Khianat could be sharp tactically and still drift in quiet positions.

Stockfish's rating limiter runs on its own scale, not a human one, and even a forty game match leaves an uncertainty of well over a hundred points — which is why every Elo figure here carries its confidence interval. The solve rates and match scores are measured; everything derived from them is an orientation. Khianat has never played a rated game against a human.
